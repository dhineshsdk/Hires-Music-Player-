# StaaaaandBy integration research

Research date: 15 November 2026. Scope: planning only; no application source changed, no donor build/scripts run, no device testing performed. Source snapshot: `D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy`. Paths below are absolute and clickable; line anchors are one-based.

## What is actually implemented and worth bringing into our app?

### Takeaway

StaaaaandBy supplies a coherent visual experience: charging dock artwork, a clock, playback controls, and deterministic animated lyrics. Its external-player monitoring and persistent background launch machinery solve problems our own player does not have; adopt the presentation concepts around our existing native player instead of merging the whole application.

### Cited Findings

- The project has 18 Kotlin files, a 39-file JIZURA bundle totaling 2,138,273 bytes, three integration JS files, and one HTML entry page. The 18 Kotlin files cover setup, preferences, standby activity, three lyric components, five media components, two charging components, three Spotify components, and two UI components. Standby keeps the screen on, turns it on and shows above the lock screen; it closes after a 1.5-second unplug grace, extended to three seconds when full, to accommodate charging pauses documented by the author on Samsung hardware. - **Slit-scan artwork:** native Compose Canvas draws an album-art square plus a stretched one-pixel column in landscape, or row in portrait. The sampled row/column travels with playback progress; clipping and scale/translation give subpixel motion. Fullscreen gestures divide the screen into thirds for previous/play-pause/next; absent now-playing info makes any tap dismiss. Clock/date/battery and marquee title/artist sit above the artwork. - **Visual states:** a lyric lookup pending for the current track hides slit-scan art; matched timed lyrics switch to the WebView; missing lyrics restore artwork. No metadata yields a clock screen, dimmed to 5% at the Activity level. Layout changes between portrait/landscape and lyric/artwork presentations. Artwork text contrast is chosen using whole-image average luminance; the lyric palettes separately target 7:1 text and 4.5:1 accents. - **Lyric repository:** current/up-next flows trigger on-demand LRCLIB `/get`, then `/search` fallback. Requests include title, artist, album and duration; fallback tolerates four seconds difference. It tries both full and first-listed artist. It stores two 32-entry in-memory LRUs (exact and loose match), prefetches next track, writes a 300-file disk LRU to `cacheDir/lyrics`, and caches misses for seven days on disk. Responses currently only use `syncedLyrics`; no plain or word-level display is implemented here. Local files use `WebViewAssetLoader` at `appassets.androidplatform.net`; native code sends JSON objects with song, colors and playback anchors. JS extrapolates from `performance.now()` and draws a pure time-indexed plan; it does not carry the music playback stream through Web Audio. Bundled generic JIZURA audio-analysis/export modules exist but the dock integration does not call those entry points. JS starts with 60 fps/DPR cap 2 and drops to 30 fps/DPR 1 after two slow ten-second windows, recovering after three fast windows. When paused it avoids redrawing an unchanged time, although `requestAnimationFrame` continues. Author-reported 110+ fps without effects is a donor comment, not an S24+ benchmark. ### Inferences

- Best integration boundary: a new display layer consumes our own stable track ID, metadata, artwork URI/bitmap, duration, playback position anchor, playing/buffering/ended state, speed and available transport commands. Share the lyric repository with the normal Playing screen. Keep decoder, DSP, routing, queue ownership and MediaSession unchanged.
- Retain the five approved tabs. Offer **Dock Mode** from Playing/its overflow, with a fullscreen exit and settings under appearance. It should be a mode of the current player, not a sixth tab or another playback service.
- Reuse/adapt the native slit-scan geometry and palette ideas first. Use our typography and Obsidian Gold/Tube Amber palette; allow subtle art-derived accents inside Dock Mode rather than replacing the whole app theme.
- MIT JIZURA can later be an isolated optional renderer; native Compose line lyrics should remain the low-cost, accessible and offline fallback. Feed only metadata, trusted lyric text, colors and timing; never pass PCM, USB handles or credentials into the WebView.

### Gaps

- All Kotlin component responsibilities and main integration paths were examined; the 2.14 MB engine was inspected structurally through its entry points, planning/render contract, font/network and unused audio/export surfaces. Every visual pack's individual animation/math implementation was not audited line by line. Do not claim complete runtime verification or exhaustive security review.
- No APK build, visual playback, device power/temperature measurement or automated test execution was done. Donor currently has build CI rather than evidence of an audio/visual regression suite.

## Which donor behavior should we change, defer or exclude?

### Takeaway

The valuable feature is an immersive display for our player, not being a universal Spotify companion. Avoid importing broad permissions and brittle heuristics into a commercial hi-res application; strengthen lifecycle, matching, caching, accessibility and WebView containment before shipping the visuals.

### Cited Findings

- The donor manifest requests notification-listener access, `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, boot handling and a separate `specialUse` foreground watcher. The notification listener itself is empty; its role is granting access to third-party active MediaSessions. [Manifest](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:5), [Empty listener](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/NowPlayingListenerService.kt:5)
- The watcher filters sessions through a hard-coded list of ten music packages; our `com.audiophile.engine` package is not included. Therefore simply bundling its observer would not show our player. Queue fallback guesses the next item as the first title different from the current title when the active queue index is unknown. [Allowlist](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:301), [Session filter](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:389), [Queue heuristic](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:115)
- External-player reliability uses service binding to MediaRoute2ProviderService/MediaBrowserService, global `AudioManager.isMusicActive` remote inference, a 15-second watchdog, track-end overrun and a 30-second remote pause test. Suspected desync hides metadata and suppresses taps. Automatic foreground resync code remains present but is disabled (`AUTO_RESYNC_ENABLED=false`). These are defensive guesses, not authoritative playback state. [Keepalive](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MusicAppKeepAlive.kt:28), [Watchdog](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:223), [Resync flag](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:292)
- Optional Spotify auth uses browser Authorization Code + PKCE, state comparison, a refresh mutex, and a custom callback URI. Access/refresh tokens and PKCE state are stored in ordinary app-private SharedPreferences; app backup is enabled in the manifest. This is not proof of token exposure, but backup exclusions and a credential-storage design need explicit review for reuse. [PKCE](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/spotify/SpotifyAuth.kt:70), [Token preferences](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/Prefs.kt:104), [Backup](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:30)
- Hybrid Spotify normally verifies every 45 seconds (60 idle), falls back to four-second polling when session/API disagree, slows paused polling to 10/30 seconds, compares title/duration/play status/position, and returns to MediaSession when they agree. `429` retry state persists across restart. It is a real separate remote-control subsystem, not needed for our local playback. [Hybrid](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/spotify/HybridPlaybackSource.kt:50), [Rate limits](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/spotify/SpotifyAuth.kt:169), [Web player](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/spotify/SpotifyWebPlayer.kt:75)
- Static analysis found several reuse hardening needs: artwork decoding has no pixel-dimension limit; the MediaSession URL path uses `URL.openStream()` without explicit timeouts; lyrics response uses unbounded `readBytes()`; cache writes are direct, without atomic rename; WebView requests outside local assets are not explicitly denied; `onPageFinished` marks ready without a JS handshake; script failure has no native fallback. These are exposed design gaps, not demonstrated exploits. [Artwork](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:537), [Network art](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/ArtLoader.kt:12), [Lyric fetch](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/lyrics/LyricsRepository.kt:246), [Cache write](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/lyrics/LyricsDiskCache.kt:49), [WebView setup](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/ui/LyricVideo.kt:111)
- Lyrics errors are caught and remembered as misses, so a temporary network error can become a seven-day negative disk entry. Identity is title+artist+whole-second duration, allowing collisions between releases. Prefetch and current lookup can issue separate requests. [Error/miss conflation](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/lyrics/LyricsRepository.kt:130), [Identity](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/media/MediaSessionWatcher.kt:43), [Cache TTL](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/java/com/kazuto/standby/lyrics/LyricsDiskCache.kt:22)
- Fonts are requested lazily from Google Fonts for the chosen plan; this prevents guaranteeing the same typography on first-launch offline. The HTML and JS explicitly contain those external origins. [HTML](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/assets/lyric/index.html:7), [Font loader](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/app/src/main/assets/lyric/jizura/02_fonts.js:80)
- Android restricts background Activity launches. The current documentation lists user-granted overlay access as an exception but recommends user-driven entry and covers newer PendingIntent opt-ins. A running foreground service alone is not a general permission to show fullscreen UI. [Android Activity security](https://developer.android.com/guide/components/activities/secure-bal)
- `specialUse` FGS requires a declared use-case explanation and Play review. `mediaPlayback` is for continuing media playback; it should not be used to disguise a permanent charging monitor. [Android FGS types](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/changes/fgs-types-required), [Service purpose](https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/service-types)
- Android's `DreamService` explicitly supports interactive screensavers for idle charging/docked devices, with window/dream lifecycle hooks and a system settings entry. It is a plausible later alternative to the donor's overlay/background-Activity machinery. [DreamService](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/service/dreams/DreamService)
- Spotify's developer policy bars synchronizing its sound recordings with visual media and restricts mixing/overlapping Spotify audio. The donor README itself labels this personal-use source and warns about combining Spotify and lyric video. Do not assume an MIT renderer license authorizes synchronizing Spotify recordings or distributing third-party lyric content. [Spotify policy III.6–7](https://developer.spotify.com/policy), [Donor README](D:/MusicPlayer-Projects/StaaaaandBy/README.md)
- LRCLIB's current API requires client identification and respecting `Retry-After`, advises sequential requests with spacing, and offers optional registration/public app IDs. `/get` duration is limited to 1–3600 seconds and matches within two seconds; current records can include word-level timing. The donor's line-only parser and potentially concurrent prefetch should be reevaluated against this current API. [Official API documentation](https://lrclib.net/docs.txt)
- LRCLIB server code is MIT, but the inspected API documentation does not grant a blanket license to lyrics. A currently open issue asks the maintainers exactly about commercial lyrics rights; it contains a question, not permission. [Server repository](https://github.com/tranxuanthang/lrclib), [Unanswered commercial-use question](https://github.com/tranxuanthang/lrclib/issues/134)

### Inferences

- Exclude external-package scanning/listening, keepalive binding, Spotify polling/auth and silent relaunch from the initial integrated feature. This removes entire classes of stale mirrors and account controls while retaining everything needed for our own playback.
- Launch manually while unlocked first. Offer optional in-app suggestion when charging in landscape; do not wake a locked screen by default. DreamService should be a separate later capability test on S24+/One UI, not a promise of guaranteed automatic launch. Do not claim access to Samsung's system AOD.
- Strengthen lyrics into explicit states: disabled, loading, synced, plain, instrumental, missing, offline, rate-limited and failed. Keep artwork visible during lookup; only genuine missing records get a negative-cache TTL. Use stable local track IDs plus release metadata and persisted manual overrides.
- Make source priority local-first: embedded lyrics → adjacent user-imported LRC/TTML when available through granted storage → user-selected file → cache → optional network provider. Honor offset fields and preserve true timing granularity. Do not fabricate word synchronization from line-only LRC.
- Cache immutable parsed lyric documents; choose native line display and optional lyric theatre as two renderers over the same document. Debounce seeks without delaying transport, gate results by current track generation, and sample position using monotonic anchors. Free/freeze renderer resources on stop; never poll JNI at display refresh rate.
- Treat OAuth and lyrics distribution as separate release decisions. A registration authorization document for API access does not itself establish copyright permission to redistribute all fetched lyrics.
- Our own native audio callback remains untouched; visual work stays on UI/worker threads. Use bounded artwork decode, bounded lyric response, atomic cache writes, request coalescing, global provider backoff, local font assets with notices and WebView origin/content restrictions. Remove unused JIZURA audio/export code only after checking registry dependencies.

### Gaps

- No S24+ device was available to confirm DreamService settings discovery, One UI charging/lock behavior, USB hotplug, rendering power cost or thermal limits. Those must be explicit tests, not inherited assurances from Flip7 comments.
- Lyrics-content commercial rights need a documented grant or an appropriate provider arrangement before shipping that online feature. No reliable blanket rights grant was located; server MIT status is insufficient.
- Static code concerns above were not reproduced as failures. Before code import, a focused correctness/security pass should confirm each and avoid labeling hypothetical issues as known vulnerabilities.

## What should the phased plan and product brainstorm look like?

### Takeaway

Deliver a polished manual Dock Mode first, backed by our own session. Add a shared offline-first lyric layer next, then a controlled optional kinetic renderer, with automatic screensaver integration only after measured S24+ behavior supports it.

Product ideas worth prototyping, without committing all of them:

1. **Gallery:** album square plus subtle progress slit; art hue lights a thin amber perimeter. Small truthful route/format label can be shown, but no invented levels.
2. **Reading room:** large calm native lyric line, previous/next context and translation toggle if supported/authorized; serif title, mono clock and clear controls, reduced-motion by default at night.
3. **Lyric theatre:** curated kinetic typography only for tracks with valid timed text. Avoid donor horror/Japanese novelty styles by default; provide a consistent luxury palette. Real line/word timing determines animation, not a simulated beat meter.
4. **Night clock:** nearly black background, warm dim clock with slowly varied placement, optional art thumbnail, no persistent bright gold bars. Call it a normal app display, not low-power system AOD, and do not promise burn-in prevention.
5. **Album session:** show album side/track position, upcoming track and total remaining queue time from actual queue data; keep the same playback engine and gapless boundaries.
6. **Desk controls:** large optional visible transport buttons and a deliberate control lock prevent accidental bedside taps. Retain accessibility semantics and keyboard/switch/TalkBack operation; invisible thirds alone are unsuitable as the only controls.
7. **Private dock:** offer hide titles/artwork over lock screen, clock only when paused, and explicit locked-screen transport permission preference. Do not expose a private library automatically.
8. **Performance choice:** Quiet / Balanced / Theatre visual settings independent of audio quality. Lower visual resolution/cadence or fall back to native art under heat; never silently change resampling, bit depth or DSP to support animation.

Suggested test matrix: portrait/landscape and both rotations; charging wireless/wired/unplug/full-charge cycles; screen on/off and side-key dismissal; player stopped/paused/buffering/ended; queue end/repeat/shuffle/seek; long classical pieces over one hour; no art/huge art; local vs Drive vs Telegram while offline/slow network; missing/stale/corrupt lyric cache; RTL/Indic/CJK/emoji and font scaling; accessibility/reduced motion; USB DAC insert/removal and Bluetooth route changes; app background/foreground/process recreation. Audio integrity checks must confirm identical source/output formats and no added xruns when the display starts/stops.

### Gaps

- Do not attach calendar estimates until the baseline app's actual build/test state and selected first slice are established. Relative size: native manual Dock medium, lyric data layer medium, kinetic renderer medium-to-large, robust automatic locked-screen behavior high uncertainty.
- Native lyric renderer vs JIZURA should be selected by prototypes and measurements, not visual ambition alone. The plan supports both without doubling lyric fetching or owning playback twice.
