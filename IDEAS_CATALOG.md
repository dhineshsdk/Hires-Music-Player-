## 1. Flagship DAP & Circuit Emulation Suite

### A. Sony Signature Sound Engine (DMP-Z1 & NW-WM1ZM2)
* **Sony DC Phase Linearizer (6 Presets):**
  * Approximates the low-frequency phase characteristics of classic analog bipolar junction transistor (BJT) and field-effect transistor (FET) power amplifiers.
  * Presets: *Type A Low*, *Type A Standard* ($32\text{ Hz}$ knee), *Type A High*, *Type B Low*, *Type B Standard*, *Type B High*.
  * Restores analog low-end punch and acoustic body without artificially boosting bass amplitude.
* **Sony Vinyl Processor (4 Sub-Engines):**
  * *Tonearm Low-Frequency Resonance:* $11\text{ Hz}$ subsonic harmonic warmth generated by cartridge compliance and tonearm mass interaction.
  * *Turntable Platter Mechanical Drag:* Authentic rotational friction and motor bearing rumble spectrum.
  * *Stereo Acoustic Crosstalk:* Realistic $-30\text{ dB}$ frequency-dependent channel bleed simulating stylus-in-groove physical tracing.
  * *Micro-Surface Drag Noise:* Subtle, non-intrusive high-frequency vinyl surface friction.
* **DSEE Ultimate (Digital Sound Enhancement Engine):**
  * High-frequency acoustic restoration algorithm analyzing spectral roll-off and predicting missing overtone harmonics ($20\text{ kHz} - 48\text{ kHz}$) lost during lossy compression or 16-bit brickwall truncation.

### B. Astell&Kern Teraton Alpha Architecture
* **DAR (Digital Audio Remaster) Engine:**
  * Real-time integer-multiple upsampling for ultra-clean phase behavior:
    * $44.1\text{ kHz} \rightarrow 352.8\text{ kHz}$ (8x integer oversampling)
    * $48.0\text{ kHz} \rightarrow 384.0\text{ kHz}$ (8x integer oversampling)
  * Real-time 5th-Order CIFF PCM-to-DSD256 Modulator for DACs with dedicated DSD analog FIR filters.
* **Teraton Noise-Gate & Discrete Stage Simulator:**
  * Simulates Astell&Kern's SP3000 quad-DAC architecture with independent analog buffer stages, eliminating power supply ripple and cross-stage interference.

### C. HiBy MSEB (MageSound 8-Ball) Psychoacoustic Tuning
* Replaces raw decibel equalizer sliders with direct psychoacoustic perception controls:
  1. *Sound Temperature:* Cool (analytical, crisp) $\longleftrightarrow$ Warm (lush, rich)
  2. *Bass Extension:* Sub-bass reach ($20\text{ Hz} - 40\text{ Hz}$)
  3. *Bass Texture:* Fast & punchy $\longleftrightarrow$ Deep & resonant
  4. *Note Thickness:* Thin & airy $\longleftrightarrow$ Dense & weighty
  5. *Vocal Position:* Forward & intimate $\longleftrightarrow$ Recessed & expansive
  6. *Female Sibilance:* Crispness vs. soft de-essing ($6\text{ kHz} - 8\text{ kHz}$)
  7. *Treble Crispness:* Brilliant air ($10\text{ kHz} - 18\text{ kHz}$)
  8. *Impulse Transient Response:* Snappy attack vs. relaxed decay

### D. Physical Vacuum Tube Laboratory (Koren Non-Linear ODE Modeling)
* **Physical Triode & Pentode Emulation:**
  * **12AX7 / ECC83 Triode:** Classic high-mu preamp tube, dominant second-harmonic even-order warmth, soft clipping saturation.
  * **6SN7 Dual Triode:** Octal-base vintage preamp valve, wide holographic soundstage, open airy midrange.
  * **300B Directly Heated Triode (DHT):** The holy grail of single-ended triodes (SET), lush vocal presence, expansive three-dimensional depth.
  * **EL34 Power Pentode:** British rock punch, assertive upper-midrange bite, dynamic power-stage compression.
  * **KT88 Beam Power Tetrode:** High headroom, ultra-fast transients, visceral low-frequency impact.
* **Jiles-Atherton Output Transformer Hysteresis:**
  * Mathematical physical model of ferromagnetic core saturation and magnetic domain memory, adding authentic low-end density below $25\text{ Hz}$.

### E. Vintage Discrete R2R Resistor Ladder Simulation (NOS vs. OS)
* **Burr-Brown PCM1704 / Philips TDA1541A Ladder Models:**
  * Monte Carlo Gaussian tolerance mismatch ($\sigma_R = 0.01\% - 0.5\%$) generating real non-monotonic resistor step transitions.
  * Inter-bit switching glitch current spikes on major carry transitions (e.g., $01111111 \rightarrow 10000000$).
* **Non-Oversampling (NOS) Mode:**
  * Bypasses digital oversampling filters, allowing natural analog sinc roll-off ($-3.17\text{ dB}$ at Nyquist) with time-domain impulse response and zero pre-ringing/post-ringing.

### F. Master Studio Tape Machine Emulation
* **Studer A820 1/2" 30 IPS Mastering Deck:**
  * Selectable tape formulations: *Formula 456* (warm tape compression) and *GP9* (high output, clean transients).
  * Authentic head-bump bass resonance ($45\text{ Hz} - 65\text{ Hz}$), tape bias saturation, and adjustable wow & flutter.

## 3. Real-Time Forensic Stream Auditor & Psychoacoustics

### A. 24-Bit Dynamic Bit-Depth Activity Ladder
* Continuous 24-column LED activity meter tracking active bit toggles from MSB (Bit 24) down to LSB (Bit 1).
* **Fake 24-Bit Detection:** Flags truncated or padded containers where LSBs remain completely dead (`0x00`) while upper bits toggle.

### B. Nyquist Ultrasonic Energy Auditor (Fake Hi-Res Detector)
* Real-time FFT spectral energy ratio calculation:
  $$\text{Ratio} = \frac{\int_{22.05\text{ kHz}}^{f_s/2} |X(f)|^2 df}{\int_{0}^{22.05\text{ kHz}} |X(f)|^2 df}$$
* If $\text{Ratio} < 10^{-7}$ on a $96\text{ kHz}$ or $192\text{ kHz}$ file, triggers the **"UPSAMPLED FAKE HI-RES"** warning banner.

### C. ISO 226:2023 Dynamic Equal-Loudness System
* Continuously calculates the human ear's equal-loudness phon contour based on real-time master output volume.
* Dynamically boosts bass ($85\text{ Hz}$) and treble air ($9\text{ kHz}$) at low listening volumes to preserve full, lifelike tonal balance without manual EQ adjustment.

### D. LR4 Missing Fundamental Virtual Sub-Bass Synthesizer
* Splits audio below $45\text{ Hz}$ via a Linkwitz-Riley 4th-order (LR4) crossover.
* Generates second- and third-order non-linear harmonics ($90\text{ Hz}$ and $135\text{ Hz}$) using Chebyshev polynomials, allowing tiny IEM drivers and headphones to reproduce visceral deep bass pitch perception that physical drivers cannot mechanically output.
